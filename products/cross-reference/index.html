How to cross-reference
  • By cutout. 4.3, 7, 10.1, 12.1 and 15 inch. Give Russ the panel cutout you have and he will tell you which bezel drops in, or have one made that does.
  • By I/O count. Fixed and expandable PLCs to 16 modules; HMI/PLC units with the I/O on the back.
  • By protocol. Serial, Ethernet, Modbus RTU and TCP, MQTT where the data has to leave the machine.
  • By what changes in the program. Russ will say, before you order, what has to be rewritten and what does not.
