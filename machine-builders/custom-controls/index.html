What can be built to your print
  • Your logo on the bezel. Printed or moulded, at production quantity, not as a one-off favour.
  • Panel cutout to your dimensions. The opening you already have, so a replacement drops in.
  • Your mounting pattern. Hole spacing and fixings to match the machine, not the catalog.
  • The I/O count you need. Discrete and analog points sized to the machine, with Ethernet, serial or Modbus as the application asks.
  • One part number for the lineup. Approved once, carried across every model that shares the panel.
